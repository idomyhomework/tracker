# Mandala is standalone in v1

The Mandala Module does not read from or write to Gym, Diet or Habits in v1: a Mandala's Actions are progressed only by the user's own hand. We chose this to keep the module simple and the product shippable, even though automatic links (e.g. an Action driven by a Gym Program) are the obvious "unique" feature. A future reader should not assume the absence of links is an oversight.
