# 10: Past Programs

**What to build:** When the user switches to a new Program, the previous one moves to Past Programs, where the user sees when each was started and last trained and can restart one as the active Program. Workout history of archived Programs is kept.

- [ ] Switching is not possible during an Active Workout.
